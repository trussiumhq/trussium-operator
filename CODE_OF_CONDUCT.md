# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Trussium community is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and
professional environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds
- Give and receive feedback professionally
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people
- Respect privacy and confidentiality
- Help keep discussions relevant and productive

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Threats or encouragement of harm
- Unwanted sexual attention
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions
- Repeated bad-faith participation
- Abuse of reporting or moderation processes

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Responses may include:

- A private warning
- Temporary participation restrictions
- Removal from project spaces
- A permanent ban for severe or repeated violations

## Reporting

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately through an appropriate private GitHub
channel.

Do not use public issues to report sensitive conduct concerns.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
